There’s a category of information I check multiple times a day, but never at the right time.

Things like GitHub reviews, or something as simple as how far into the year we are.
None of this is urgent, but it’s not irrelevant either. It sits somewhere in between — important enough to check, not important enough to keep open.

I’ve been wanting a way to move this kind of information out of the browser entirely.
Not into another app. Not into a dashboard. Just… somewhere else.
